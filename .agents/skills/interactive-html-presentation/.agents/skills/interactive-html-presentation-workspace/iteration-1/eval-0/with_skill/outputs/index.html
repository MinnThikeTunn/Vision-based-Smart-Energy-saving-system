Act I · Architecture
Attention Is All You Need

Core Self-Attention

The core engine powering the Transformer architecture. Instead of processing tokens sequentially, self-attention maps relationships across the entire sequence simultaneously.
$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$$

Q · Query

Represents the current token looking for context from other parts of the sequence.

K · Key

Represents all tokens in the sequence, offering clues or indices for matching.

V · Value

Contains the actual content or information corresponding to each token.

Act I · The Bottleneck
Context Over Distance

Why Self-Attention?

Sequential Bottleneck

RNNs process words step-by-step. Information from early tokens decays, creating a vanishing gradient problem over long sentences.

O(1) Path Length

Self-attention connects any two tokens with a single operation, regardless of distance. Long-range dependencies are resolved instantly.

Parallel Training

Since relationships are computed in parallel matrices, GPUs can process entire sequences simultaneously, bypassing the recurrence limit.

Act II · The Scaling Factor
Preventing Saturation

Why Divide by $\sqrt{d_k}$?

For large values of $d_k$ (dimension size), dot products grow extremely large in magnitude. This pushes the softmax function into regions with extremely small gradients.
$$\text{Softmax}(x)_i = \frac{e^{x_i}}{\sum_j e^{x_j}}$$

By scaling by $\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_k}}$, we maintain the variance of the dot products at $1.0$, preventing the softmax from saturating (which would otherwise result in flat gradients and dead learning).

The Gradient Killer

  • Assume components of $q$ and $k$ are independent random variables with mean $0$ and variance $1$.
  • Their dot product $q \cdot k = \sum_{i=1}^{d_k} q_i k_i$ has mean $0$ and variance $d_k$.
  • Dividing by $\sqrt{d_k}$ pulls the variance back to $1.0$.
Act III · Summary
Foundations of Modern AI

Key Takeaways

Contextual Embeddings

Unlike static word embeddings (Word2Vec), self-attention creates dynamic representations that change based on surrounding words.

Foundation of LLMs

From GPT to Claude, every modern large language model relies on this exact scaled dot-product formula scaled across layers.

Quadratic Complexity

Because every token compares to every other token, self-attention requires $O(N^2)$ compute and memory space, a key area of ongoing research.
