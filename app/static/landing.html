The problem

Empty rooms still burn power

Lights, fans, and air-conditioning often stay on long after people leave. Manual switches get forgotten. Timers are blunt. Motion sensors misfire. Vision Smart Energy closes that gap with camera-based occupancy intelligence.

  • Before Always-on baselines, wasted kWh, no clear proof of savings.
  • Guesswork controls Fixed schedules ignore real room usage and zone context.
  • After Live person detection, zone-aware decisions, measurable ROI on the dashboard.

How it works

From camera to kilowatts saved

A continuous loop runs while the system is live: sense the room, decide device states, then prove the savings.

01

Detect

YOLOv8 reads the webcam (or demo feed) and counts people in real time.

  • Centroid tracking keeps IDs stable across brief dropouts
  • Optional heatmap shows where people dwell
  • Frames are processed live — not archived as a video library
02

Decide

Spatial zones and occupancy state machines choose what should stay powered.

  • Draw zones on the live feed (desk vs transit, etc.)
  • Persistence windows absorb camera flicker
  • Per-device shutdown timeouts avoid harsh instant cutoffs
03

Save

Virtual (or hardware-ready) devices ramp power while analytics track impact.

  • Async power ramps simulate realistic on/off curves
  • Live kWh, cost, and CO₂ prevented vs always-on baseline
  • Audit logs explain every automation decision

Capabilities

What’s inside the control center

Everything below is already implemented in the live dashboard — this home page only introduces it.

Live vision stage

Fullscreen-capable video feed with occupancy overlays, heatmap toggle, and interactive zone drawing for device-to-space mapping.

Virtual devices

Add lights, fans, AC, or custom loads with wattage, auto-off timeout, and ramp timing. Toggle them manually or let occupancy logic drive them.

Energy analytics

Hourly consumption vs baseline charts, cumulative savings, efficiency gain, CSV export, and 7-day trend views for facilities reporting.

Rules & settings

Tune persistence windows, empty-room timeouts, confidence thresholds, headless mode, and anonymization without touching core code.

Observability

WebSocket telemetry keeps the UI live. Prometheus metrics and audit logs support demos, debugging, and operations review.

Privacy first

Intelligence without a surveillance archive

Energy automation shouldn’t require storing people’s faces. The system is designed around ephemeral vision — process what you need, keep what you must, discard the rest.

Built-in safeguards

  • Zero frame retention — no long-term video library by design
  • Headless automation — run decisions without exposing the live feed UI
  • Canvas anonymization — optional blurring of people on the stream
  • Local-first demo path — works on your machine for classroom / lab demos
